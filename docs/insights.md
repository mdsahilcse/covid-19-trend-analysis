# What the archive shows

## Final reported totals

The Johns Hopkins time series lists **676,570,149 reported cases** and **6,881,802 reported deaths** across 201 reporting locations as of 9 March 2023.

| Reporting location | Cumulative reported cases | Cumulative reported deaths |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| United States | 103,802,702 | 1,123,836 |
| India | 44,690,738 | 530,779 |
| France | 39,866,718 | 166,176 |
| Brazil | 37,076,053 | 699,276 |

The United States, India, and France lead the archive's case totals. The United States, Brazil, and India lead its death totals. The table combines both sets of leaders.

## The timing differs by country

The combined trailing seven-day average of newly reported cases peaked at about **3.44 million per day** on 24 January 2022. India reached its highest reported case average much earlier, at **391,232 per day** on 8 May 2021. The United States reached **806,963 per day** on 15 January 2022. Select the two countries and use the daily trend with seven-day averages to see both peaks on the same scale.

## Revisions affect daily comparisons

The country-level series contains 171 negative case observations and 199 negative death observations. France recorded the largest negative case correction, **−348,840** on 20 May 2021. Retaining these revisions preserves the reconciliation between the daily changes and the published cumulative series.

Cases match the publisher's final daily snapshot across all 201 locations. Deaths match across 200 locations. The archived time series gives the Netherlands **23,705** deaths, while the daily snapshot gives **23,707**. The analysis uses the archived series consistently and records the two-death difference in its quality report.
